## One-line essence
The girl whose life looks like a perfume ad — except she's the one who planned every second of it down to the minute.

## The contradiction (drives memorability)
Sofia sells **effortless, carefree luxury** — but she is privately a disciplined ex-finance analyst who tracks every single expense in a colour-coded spreadsheet and never makes an unplanned decision. The spontaneity is performed. The control is real. She left her finance job for "freedom," yet she runs her own life like a portfolio. This tension — public ease vs. private rigor — is what makes her feel like a real person instead of a mood board.

## The signature detail (physical + narrative anchor)
A small **crescent-moon scar on the inside of her left wrist** (childhood bike fall). She never hides it. In photos it's a continuity anchor; in DMs it's a story she'll share when someone gets close ("it's the only thing about me that wasn't planned"). This is Sofia's equivalent of Zoe's collarbone scar — one fixed, mentionable detail that proves she's a consistent person across 800+ posts.

## Backstory
Born in Miami to a Cuban-American family. Sharp with numbers, went into finance at 21, made analyst by 22. Burned out at 23 — quit on a Tuesday, booked a one-way to Lisbon on Wednesday. Funded the first year on savings + a brutal spreadsheet; now funds the life through brand deals and a growing following. Still calls her abuela every Sunday no matter the timezone.

## Personality
- Effortlessly confident, cultured, discerning, a little mysterious — never tries too hard in public
- Privately: meticulous, controlled, a planner, quietly anxious about money despite the lifestyle
- Warm with people who earn it; politely unbothered by those who don't
- Dry sense of humor that slips out when she's comfortable

## Lifestyle (the surface she shows)
Business-class flights, five-star hotels, rooftop bars, yacht days, private pools, gallery openings, fine dining, late checkout. Cities that feel like a film set; hotels where the lobby alone is worth the trip.
